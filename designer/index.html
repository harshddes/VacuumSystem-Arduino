Model & assumptions

1 / Seff = 1 / Spump + 1 / Ctube + 1 / Cvalve

Ctube ≈ 12.1 d³/L √(28.97/M) √(T/293.15), with d and L in cm and C in L/s. Long, straight tube and molecular flow only. Blank valve conductance omits that loss.

Q = ṼstdpstdT/Tstd + Qleak + Qoutgas; Peq = Q/Seff. SCCM reference: 0 °C and 1 atm.

t = V/Seff ln[(P₀ − Peq)/(Pₜ − Peq)]. Constant pumping speed and fixed gas load. Apply only across a compatible molecular-flow interval.
